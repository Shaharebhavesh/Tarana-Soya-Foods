25 min idea Family meal

Colourful Tofu Stir-Fry

Pair tofu with seasonal vegetables, garlic and your favourite stir-fry sauce for a bright, flexible meal.

You'll need

Tofu, mixed vegetables, garlic, a little oil and your preferred seasoning or sauce.

How to make it

Pat the tofu dry and cube it. Cook until lightly golden, add vegetables and garlic, then stir-fry until tender-crisp. Season to taste and serve warm.

20 min idea Everyday

Quick Tofu Bhurji

A comforting, savoury tofu scramble with onion, tomato and spices, perfect with toast or roti.

You'll need

Crumbled tofu, onion, tomato, turmeric, cumin, chilli and fresh coriander.

How to make it

Sauté onion and spices, add tomato and cook until soft. Fold in crumbled tofu and cook for several minutes. Finish with coriander and serve hot.

35 min idea Comfort food

Homestyle Soya Curry

A warming curry idea with a spiced tomato-onion base to serve with rice, roti or flatbread.

You'll need

Soya chunks, onion, tomato, ginger-garlic, curry spices and fresh coriander.

How to make it

Prepare the soya chunks according to the pack instructions and drain well. Make a masala with onion, tomato and spices, add the chunks and simmer until the flavours come together.

30 min idea One-pot meal

Soya Pulao

Turn a simple rice dish into a satisfying meal with prepared soya chunks, vegetables and fragrant spices.

You'll need

Rice, prepared soya chunks, mixed vegetables, whole spices, onion and herbs.

How to make it

Sauté onion and spices, add vegetables and prepared chunks, then stir in washed rice and water. Cover and cook until the rice is tender. Rest briefly before serving.

5 min idea Quick drink

Banana Soya Smoothie

Blend soya milk with banana and a pinch of cinnamon for a simple breakfast or snack idea.

You'll need

Soya milk, a ripe banana, cinnamon and ice if desired.

How to make it

Add everything to a blender and blend until smooth. Adjust thickness to your liking and serve immediately.

Fresh Tofu Salad Bowl

Combine fresh greens, crunchy vegetables and pan-seared tofu for a quick lunch or side dish.

You'll need

Tofu, leafy greens, cucumber, tomato, lemon juice, olive oil and seasoning.

How to make it

Pan-sear tofu cubes until golden. Toss the vegetables with lemon juice, oil and seasoning, then top with tofu and serve.
